  • ✓ Superior growth rates and feed efficiency
  • ✓ Excellent reproductive performance
  • ✓ Disease resistance and robustness
  • ✓ Optimal carcass quality

Multiplication Program

Parent Stock (PS)

Our Parent Stock program multiplies the superior genetics from our GP herd to produce high-performing breeding animals for farms across Myanmar.

  • ✓ High litter sizes (12-14 piglets born alive)
  • ✓ Excellent mothering ability
  • ✓ Strong milking capacity
  • ✓ Consistent performance across environments
~38,000 pigs/year

Commercial Pigs

Our commercial pigs are the result of carefully planned crossbreeding programs that maximize hybrid vigor for optimal market performance.

  • ✓ Fast growth (market weight in 150-170 days)
  • ✓ Excellent feed conversion (2.5-2.8 FCR)
  • ✓ Lean, high-quality carcasses
  • ✓ Full traceability from birth to market

Biogas Generation

We convert pig waste into valuable biogas through anaerobic digestion, creating a circular economy on our farm. This process eliminates waste while generating renewable energy for heating and electricity.

Biosecurity Excellence

Biosecurity is the cornerstone of our operations. We implement multi-layered protection including controlled access zones, vehicle disinfection stations, shower-in/shower-out protocols, and comprehensive health monitoring to keep our herd disease-free.
